## Highlights

### Seamless Fullscreen Overlay
* **Stays in Fullscreen**: Maria appears smoothly over YouTube, Netflix, or Twitch without kicking you out of fullscreen mode.
* **Auto-Pauses Media**: Temporarily pauses videos and audio while Maria is on screen, then resumes right where you left off.
* **Accidental Click Protection**: Break overlays only dismiss when you press `Escape` or when the video ends — clicks won't close it by mistake.

### Crystal-Clear 60 FPS Transparent Videos
* **5 Break Types**: Hydration (Water), Gym (Stretches), Food (Snacks), Posture Check, and Rest Break.
* **Pure Transparent Backgrounds**: Seamlessly blends into whatever website you are browsing with zero black/white borders or watermarks.
* **Synchronized Audio**: Dedicated voice cues and sound chimes tailored to every individual break type.

### Redesigned Control Panel
* **One-Click Routine Switches**: Turn any reminder on or off anytime with a simple toggle slider.
* **Settings Gear (`⚙️`) for Every Routine**:
  * Quick interval buttons (`10s`, `15m`, `30m`, `1h`, `Custom`)
  * Smooth interval slider (`10s` to `2h`)
  * Sound preview button to listen to any chime
  * Delete routine button
* **Empty State**: Easily add new reminders with a clean `+ Create Reminder` button when no routines are active.

## How to Install

### Chrome, Microsoft Edge & Brave

1. Download and extract **`MariaRemindUs - Chrome & Microsoft Edge Extension.zip`**.
2. Open your browser's extensions page:
   * **Chrome**: `chrome://extensions`
   * **Edge**: `edge://extensions`
   * **Brave**: `brave://extensions`
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (toggle in the top-right corner).
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left) and select the extracted folder.
5. Pin **MariaRemindsUs** to your toolbar and enjoy!

### Mozilla Firefox

1. Download **`MariaRemindUs - FireFox Extension.zip`**.
2. In Firefox's address bar, type `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox` and press Enter.
3. Click **Load Temporary Add-on...**.
4. Select `MariaRemindUs - FireFox Extension.zip` (or the `manifest.json` file inside the extracted folder).
5. The extension will load immediately.
