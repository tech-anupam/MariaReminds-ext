A mindful hydration & break companion for your browser.



### What Maria Does

- **Hydration Reminders** — Reminds you to drink water and take care of yourself at your chosen pace
- **Fullscreen Companionship** — Displays Maria smoothly centered on your active tab when it's time for a break
- **Smart Media Handling** — Automatically pauses YouTube, streaming players, and web audio during breaks, then resumes them after
- **Companion Audio** — Plays Maria's voice and audio prompts reliably without browser autoplay hiccups
- **Flexible Timing** — Set custom intervals in hours, minutes, or seconds with an intuitive slider and presets
- **Typing Awareness** — Waits until you pause typing so it never interrupts your workflow mid-sentence
- **Domain Whitelist** — Silence reminders on meeting or presentation sites (Zoom, Meet, Figma, etc.)
- **One-Click Dismiss** — Close anytime with a simple click or `Esc` key
- **100% Offline & Private** — Runs entirely in your browser with zero tracking or external requests
